Matchup page — design directions

Two takes on the "This Week" page, built with the league's real current data: this week's fixtures, win odds, form, luck and club badges. Tap a fixture in either one to open its full comparison.

Concept A

Matchday

  • Inspired by a top-flight league's energy, but its own identity
  • Violet, hot pink and citrus; angled cut-corner panels
  • Giant matchday numeral and lineup-lock countdown
  • Featured match, then compact fixture strips that expand
Open concept A
Concept B

Broadcast

  • Feels like a live sports telecast
  • News ticker with this week's headlines
  • Game of the week hero, scorebug fixture strips
  • Team-colour bars and a tale-of-the-tape comparison
Open concept B
Legal note on A: it borrows the general feel of a premium league broadcast (dark purple, neon accent, bold numerals) but uses no league name, lion, wordmark, official typeface or the league's own terms ("Matchday" rather than their "Matchweek"). The odds are a for-fun estimate from the official fantasy expected-points numbers, not betting advice.
