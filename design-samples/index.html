Working mockups built on the league's real current data. Tap around in each — they behave like the real thing.

Home page — three directions

All three keep the dark gold-and-plum theme and the drifting pass map, and bring in the motion and polish of the This Week and Cup pages. Clicking a manager takes you to their My Season page, just like the real Home page.
