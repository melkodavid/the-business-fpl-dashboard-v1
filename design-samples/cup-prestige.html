Traditions we could add to any of them

The themes change the look; these change the feel. Each one is a small feature that would sit on the Cup page whichever theme you pick.

The Champion's Blazer

The Cup's own jacket. The winner "wears" it on the site all year: it hangs on the Cup page with their name stitched inside, and gets handed over to next year's champion. A blazer badge appears next to their club name everywhere on the site.

Inaugural holder: to be decided at GW36

The Honours Board

Every Cup winner in gold leaf, one line per season, like the board in a clubhouse bar. Starts with this season's inaugural winner and grows every year. Could also list runners-up and the highest single score of each Cup.

The Hand-Turned Leaderboard

On cup weeks, the live scores appear on an old white leaderboard with every letter on its own tile, as if someone is climbing a ladder to change them. Ties sit in pairs, leaders' numbers turn red, and tiles flip when a score changes.
